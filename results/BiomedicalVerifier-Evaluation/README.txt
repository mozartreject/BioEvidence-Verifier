Read claims-and-quarantine-text.txt for the claims and reasons, results.csv for the run index, and raw/ for full responses. verification.json records the integrity checks. initial-v1/ preserves the earlier version, including its failures and source snapshot. source/ and system-version.json identify the repaired version.

Images are genuine dashboard renders of saved inference responses, with request IDs. They are grouped by actual output. Expected candidate labels are not an independently adjudicated ground-truth dataset; this collection does not establish medical accuracy. Read disagreements-to-review.txt and amber index rows for remaining classification disagreements and failed searches. Scores are discrete, uncalibrated AI estimates conditional on the evidence verdict. No class labels, sources or percentages were substituted.
